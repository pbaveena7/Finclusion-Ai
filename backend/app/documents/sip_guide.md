# SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a facility offered by mutual funds to investors to invest in a disciplined manner. It allows an investor to invest a fixed amount of money at pre-defined intervals in the selected mutual fund scheme.

## Key Benefits
- **Rupee Cost Averaging**: Since the amount is fixed, more units are bought when the market goes down, and fewer units when the market goes up.
- **Power of Compounding**: Regular investments over a long period build a large corpus due to compound interest.
- **Flexibility**: Investors can start SIPs with amounts as low as ₹500 per month.
- **Discipline**: It instills financial discipline by automating monthly investments.

## Best Practices
- Start early to maximize the compounding effect.
- Do not stop SIPs during market downturns, as these are the best times to accumulate units at lower NAV (Net Asset Value).
